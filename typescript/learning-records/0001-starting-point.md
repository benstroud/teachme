# Starting point established

Ben is a self-taught, productive TypeScript developer aiming for senior-level
JS/TS + Node.js mastery (driver: leveling up / interviews). He self-reports
foundational gaps *under* the advanced material and explicitly wants those filled
rather than skipped — so teaching starts at the bedrock (types as sets) and builds
toward type-level programming, not the reverse.

This sets the floor: assume fluency with day-to-day generics, interfaces, and
unions in *usage*, but do not assume he can reason about assignability, variance,
or `never`/`unknown` from first principles until demonstrated. Lesson 01 (Types as
Sets) is the diagnostic + foundation. See [[MISSION.md]].
