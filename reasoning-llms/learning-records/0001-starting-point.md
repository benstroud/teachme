# Starting Point: Reasoning vs Non-Reasoning LLMs

The learner is a self-taught TypeScript backend developer working toward senior
JS/TS + Node.js mastery who already integrates large language models into real
applications. He wants to understand the new class of "reasoning" models in
relation to standard LLMs: how standard models generate text and where they fail
on multi-step problems; how reinforcement learning trains models to think; the
role of chain-of-thought and whether it is shown or hidden; the reasoning-effort
and token/latency/cost trade-offs; what reasoning benchmarks actually measure; and
how to route real queries to a standard vs a reasoning model in production. This
lesson set is designed to be self-contained and independent of all other courses
in the repository.
