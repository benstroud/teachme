Lesson 8 of 10Reasoning vs Non-Reasoning LLMs

When Reasoning Does Not Help

Trivia, extraction, latency, cost — and the hallucination you cannot think your way out of.

The mental model in one sentence

Reasoning models are not universally better: for easy recall, extraction, and latency-sensitive calls they add cost and delay with little accuracy gain — and they still hallucinate, because thinking improves process, not the model's knowledge.1

When the standard model is the better tool

Task classWhy deliberation adds little
Trivia / fact recallThe answer is in weights; no steps to verify.
Simple classification / extractionShort, direct; extra tokens return nothing.
Latency-sensitive UIsThinking tokens delay every keystroke response.
High-volume / low-budgetReasoning costs many more tokens per call.
Key idea Reasoning is a compute trade, not a correctness guarantee. A harder thinker can still be wrong, and on easy tasks the extra thinking is pure overhead — which is why routing exists (Lesson 10).1
