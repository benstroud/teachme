Lesson 7 of 10Reasoning vs Non-Reasoning LLMs

When Reasoning Wins

Math, competitions, code, and deep analysis: where deliberation reliably lifts the score.

The mental model in one sentence

Reasoning models win where the task has long, checkable, multi-step structure — math, competitions, code, and deep analysis — where deliberation directly increases the odds of a right answer.1

The evidence on hard benchmarks

TaskStandard modelReasoning model (o1)
AIME 2024 math~12% solved (1.8/15)74% single sample; 93% with reranking1
Codeforces—~89th percentile1
GPQA (PhD science)—exceeds human PhD-level accuracy1

Why these tasks favor thinking

  • The answer can be verified, which is exactly what RL rewarded the model for achieving.
  • Progress happens step by step, so breaking it down and checking helps.
  • Long deliberation converts a lucky guess into a derived result.
Key idea Test-time compute scaling is the mechanism behind the gains: more thinking tokens (and, in research, verification/search) lift accuracy on exactly these reasoning-heavy workloads.2
