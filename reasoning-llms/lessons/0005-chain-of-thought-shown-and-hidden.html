Chain of Thought: Shown & Hidden

The deliberation you may or may not get to read, and why it is often hidden.

The mental model in one sentence

What a reasoning model "thinks" is a chain of thought produced before the reply — and providers decide whether you see the raw deliberation (DeepSeek shows it) or only a short summary (OpenAI hides it), usually for safety and competitive reasons.1

Where chain of thought came from

Chain-of-thought first became famous as a prompting technique: asking a model to "think step by step" measurably improved its answers to multi-step questions. Reasoning models internalize that idea as trained behavior rather than an ask.2

In the API

# reasoning content often appears apart from the final answer
response.reasoning_summary   # provider-controlled summary

Key idea "Hidden" does not mean absent — the model still thinks. The provider simply withholds the raw deliberation you could otherwise copy or abuse.1
