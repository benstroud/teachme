Lesson 3 of 10Reasoning vs Non-Reasoning LLMs

What a Reasoning Model Adds

Deliberate internal thought, trained in with reinforcement learning, to buy correctness on hard steps.

The mental model in one sentence

A reasoning model does not change what a model is — it changes how the model spends compute: it produces a long chain of deliberation first, and that deliberation is what lifts performance on hard, multi-step tasks.1

Training deliberation with reinforcement learning

Instead of only imitating human text, a reasoning model is trained to maximize a verifiable reward — for example, getting the final math answer right. Reinforcement learning over many generations rewards the behaviors that lead to correct answers, which is what causes long chains of thought (with self-checking and re-verification) to emerge.2

What "thinking" is made of

  • Expanding the problem into smaller sub-steps.
  • Checking an intermediate result before continuing.
  • Re-reading and correcting before writing the final answer.

These are the same moves chain-of-thought prompting asks a standard model to make — except now they are trained in as reliable behavior rather than requested.3

Key idea The gain is not a better memory of facts — it is a better process over the facts already in context. That is why reasoning models shine brightest where the steps are long and the reward is checkable.2
