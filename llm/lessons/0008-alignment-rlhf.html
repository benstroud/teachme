Alignment (RLHF)

Next-token prediction alone cannot capture "good answers to people". RLHF adds human preferences as the signal that shapes behavior.

The mental model in one sentence

Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) aligns a base model to human preferences in three steps: take a pretrained (and supervised fine-tuned) model, train a reward model from human preference data, then fine-tune the LM with reinforcement learning.1

The three steps

  1. Start with a pretrained (and often SFT) model — e.g. OpenAI used a smaller GPT-3 for InstructGPT.1
  2. Train a reward model — from human-ranked pairs of responses, model what "preferable" output looks like.1
  3. Optimize with RL — fine-tune the LM (typically with PPO) to maximize the reward, with a KL penalty to the original model so it does not drift too far.1

Why it matters

"Good" text is subjective — helpful, harmless, honest — which a next-token loss cannot express. RLHF introduces human feedback as the optimization signal, which is what made instruction-following models like ChatGPT possible.1

Key idea Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) alone teaches a style; the RLHF reward model + RL step pushes the model toward preferred behavior. The KL penalty keeps alignment from ruining what the base model learned.1
