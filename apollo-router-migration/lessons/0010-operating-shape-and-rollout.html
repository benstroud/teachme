Operating & Rolling Out

Telemetry, traffic shaping, caching — and a reversible cutover plan.

The mental model in one sentence

Once migrated, the router is operated through configuration you review like code: OpenTelemetry telemetry, traffic shaping, and caching — plus a roll-out plan that can be reversed.1

What you get out of the box

ConcernRouter feature
ObservabilityOpenTelemetry traces & metrics (Datadog, Prometheus, …)1
ResilienceTraffic shaping: timeouts, rate limiting on router/subgraph1
PerformanceQuery batching, APQ, Redis-backed response caching1

A reference migration shape

compose supergraph (CI)
     |
     v
router (--supergraph in CI, artifact/Uplink in prod)
  + router.yaml: propagation, cors, telemetry, traffic_shaping
  + Rhai / coprocessors for custom logic
     |
proxy in front  ->  canary % to router, rest to gateway
     |
watch OTEL traces + error codes, then cut over
Key idea The router makes the gateway's operational knobs declarative and observable. Treat router.yaml as production code: review it, version it, and watch its telemetry after every change.2
