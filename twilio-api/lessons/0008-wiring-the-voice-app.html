Wiring the Voice App End-to-End

Token, app, handler, number — making the four SIDs agree.

The mental model in one sentence

Four pieces must line up: the Access Token names the TwiML app, the app points at /twiml, and the owned number is bound to the same app for inbound calls.1

The wiring

Access Token
  └─ VoiceGrant.outgoingApplicationSid ─┐
TwiML App (voice URL = /twiml) <────────┘
  └─ returns <Dial callerId=owned to=To>
IncomingPhoneNumber.voiceApplicationSid ──> same TwiML App

The checklist

PieceCheck
TokenHas identity + VoiceGrant with the app SID.1
TwiML appVoice URL points at your public /twiml.2
HandlerValidates the signature, then dials To.2
NumberOwned, with voiceApplicationSid set.3
Key idea Most "it doesn't work" bugs in this flow are a mismatched SID: the token, the app, and the number silently point at different apps. Log the SIDs at startup and keep them in one config.3
