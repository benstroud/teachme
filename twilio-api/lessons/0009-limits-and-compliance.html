Limits & Compliance

CPS, concurrency, the 4-hour cap, and outbound trust.

The mental model in one sentence

Twilio throttles calls by account: about 1 call per second by default (requests above that are queued), a cap on concurrent calls, and a 4-hour maximum per call.1

The numbers to design around

LimitDefault / behavior
Calls per second~1 CPS per account or subaccount; excess is queued.1
Concurrent callsLimited per account; raise with support as needed.1
Call durationAt most 4 hours.1

Compliance for outbound voice

  • Trusted calling (SHAKEN/STIR) authenticates caller ID for outbound calls.2
  • US outbound rules apply to how and when you may call.2
  • High-volume senders need to plan capacity and reputation.3
Key idea Queueing hides bursts from your code but not from your users: if you fan out many calls at once, expect the extra calls to wait. Design retries and user feedback around CPS, not around "it returned 200".1
