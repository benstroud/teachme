Phone Numbers: Search & Buy

Find a candidate, purchase it, and bind it to your TwiML app.

The mental model in one sentence

Buying a number is two steps: search AvailablePhoneNumber for something that fits, then purchase an IncomingPhoneNumber and bind it to your TwiML app.1

Search, then buy

// 1) search US local numbers (also toll-free)
const matches = await client
  .availablePhoneNumbers("US")
  .local.list({ areaCode: 415, limit: 5 });

// 2) purchase one and point it at your TwiML app
const number = await client.incomingPhoneNumbers.create({
  phoneNumber: matches[0].phoneNumber,
  voiceApplicationSid: process.env.TWILIO_TWIML_APP_SID,
});

What each resource is

ResourceMeaning
AvailablePhoneNumberA candidate you can buy.1
AvailablePhoneNumberLocalLocal numbers for a country/area code.2
IncomingPhoneNumberA number your account now owns.3
Key idea Search returns candidates; only the purchase creates ownership and bills you. Setting voiceApplicationSid at purchase time is what makes inbound calls hit your handler.3
