# Parade Puppet Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _68 Ways to Make Really Big Puppets_ — Sara Peattie / The Puppeteers' Cooperative](https://breadandpuppetpress.org/products/68-ways-to-make-really-big-puppets)
  The foundational pattern book for parade-scale puppetry. Covers backpack frames, simple puppets, birds, dragons, giant puppets, costumes, and head attachment. Published by Bread & Puppet Press. Use for: any structural or rigging question, backpack frame construction, head attachment methods.
  Also available on [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/68waystomakereal00peat). Free PDF excerpt at [puppetco-op.org](https://puppetco-op.org/68waystall.pdf).

- [Manual: _Giant Puppet Making 101 Building Manual_ — Appalachian Puppet Pageant / Cattywampus Puppet Council](https://cattywampuspuppetcouncil.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/2020-giant-puppet-making-manual.pdf)
  Comprehensive free PDF covering planning, papier-mache technique (cornstarch goop recipe), armatures (bucket, paper, cardboard, clay), painting, backpack frame rigging, hands, and materials sourcing. Use for: step-by-step papier-mache, materials list, backpack frame basics, beginner-friendly instructions.

- [Booklet: _Simple and Cheap Recipes for Puppet Making_ — Antoinette Martens / Bread & Puppet Press](https://breadandpuppetpress.org/products/simple-and-cheap-recipes)
  Classic 13-page booklet covering paste recipes, puppet construction basics, backdrops, and publicity. Originally 1973, updated 2020. Use for: paste/adhesive recipes, the Bread & Puppet philosophy of cheap art.

- [Tutorial: _Giant Backpack Puppets_ — Instructables](https://www.instructables.com/Giant-Backpack-Puppets/)
  Step-by-step photo tutorial for building backpack puppets. Use for: visual reference of frame construction, beginner-accessible build process.

- [Guide: _How to Make a Life-Size Puppet That Actually Moves_ — My Painted Dreams](https://mypainteddreams.com/how-to-make-a-life-size-puppet/)
  Detailed 8-step guide covering prototyping, wearable frames, torsos, heads, expression, arms, costuming, and balance testing. Use for: the full build sequence, practical tips on weight distribution and visibility.

- [PDF: _The Puppet_ — Sherry Clark / Simple Organic Solutions](https://www.simpleorganicsolutions.com/pupConstruct.pdf)
  Detailed PVC-based puppet skeleton construction guide. Covers frame pack fitting, spine options (PVC vs carbon fiber), articulating joints with shock cord, adding bulk, hair/feathers, hands. Use for: PVC skeleton engineering, joint construction, advanced frame fitting.

- [Video: _How to Build a Giant Parade Puppet Lantern_ — Bish Bosh / B.ARTS Studio](https://thekidshouldseethis.com/post/bishbosh-puppet-lantern-stoke-lanterns-festival)
  Workshop video showing willow-and-tissue construction of a giant puppet lantern. Demonstrates intersecting circle technique for rigid structures. Use for: willow construction, lantern puppets, structural principles with natural materials.

- [Blog: _Dressing a Giant_ — Delicate Stitches](http://delicatestitches.blogspot.com/2014/09/dressing-giant.html)
  Detailed costuming walkthrough for a willow-framed parade puppet. Covers construction of bodice, skirts, and finishing. Use for: costuming techniques at puppet scale.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [The Puppeteers' Cooperative / The Puppet Library — Boston, MA](http://puppetco-op.org/)
  Founded 1976 by Sara Peattie. Operates The Puppet Library from Emmanuel Church in Boston. Puppets available for protests, parades, and processionals. The original community for parade puppet making.

- [Bread and Puppet Theater — Glover, VT](https://breadandpuppet.org/)
  Founded 1963 by Peter Schumann. The seminal political puppet theater. Holds summer apprenticeships and community circuses. The deepest well of parade puppet tradition in North America.

- [Cattywampus Puppet Council — Knoxville, TN](https://cattywampuspuppetcouncil.wordpress.com/)
  Community puppet council running the Appalachian Puppet Pageant. Produces the Giant Puppet Making 101 manual. Active community parade puppet group.

- [Puppeteers of America](https://www.puppeteers.org/)
  National organization for puppetry. Hosts festivals, publishes resources, connects practitioners.

## Gaps

- No resource yet on wind resistance and weather-proofing for outdoor parade use
- No resource on puppet storage and transport logistics
