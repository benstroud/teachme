# Mission: Backpack Parade Puppets

## Why
Build large, wearable "backpack" parade puppets — the kind where an oversized character rides on a frame worn on the puppeteer's back — starting as a personal creative hobby and growing toward participating in community parades and festivals.

## Success looks like
- Design and build a complete backpack puppet from scratch using accessible materials
- Understand the structural engineering: frame, weight distribution, movement mechanisms
- Create a puppet sturdy and visually striking enough to perform in a real parade
- Feel confident enough to eventually teach others or join a puppet-building community

## Constraints
- No prior puppet-making or advanced crafting experience
- No hard deadline; learning at own pace
- Budget and tool access not yet established (assume modest home-workshop level)

## Out of scope
- Marionettes, hand puppets, shadow puppets, or other non-parade puppet forms
- Professional theatrical puppet fabrication (animatronics, silicone casting, etc.)
- Performance technique and choreography (for now — may revisit later)
