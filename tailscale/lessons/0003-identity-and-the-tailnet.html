Identity & the Tailnet

Users, devices, and tags — and why identity, not IP, is the boundary.

The mental model in one sentence

In Tailscale the security boundary is identity, not the network: you log in with your identity provider, each device joins the tailnet under a user or a tag, and policy decides what it may reach.1

Users, devices, and tags

ConceptWhat it is
UserA person identity from your identity provider (SSO).1
DeviceA machine joined under a user, until it is tagged.
TagA role label (e.g. tag:server) that replaces user ownership for servers.1

Joining and lifecycle

  • An interactive login authorizes the device the first time it joins.
  • Device keys expire by default, which is good for laptops and bad for always-on servers — disable expiry for those.
  • Server and service machines are usually given a tag so policy targets the role, not a person.
Key idea Because identity drives access, revoking a user in your identity provider cuts off their devices — no shared secret to rotate. That is the shift from network trust to zero trust.1
