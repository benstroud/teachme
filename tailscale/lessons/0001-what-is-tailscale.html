What Tailscale Is

A mesh VPN on WireGuard: identity-based, zero-config, and direct where possible.

The mental model in one sentence

Tailscale is a mesh VPN built on WireGuard: instead of routing all your traffic through one central gateway, every device gets a private overlay address and can connect directly to every other — and access is decided by identity, not by which network you are on.1

Mesh vs hub-and-spoke

Traditional VPNTailscale
TopologyHub-and-spoke: everything through one gatewayMesh: device-to-device direct connections1
SetupPorts, firewall rules, IP planningZero-config across NAT and firewalls1
TrustBeing on the network implies accessIdentity-based, zero-trust, end-to-end encrypted1

Why "zero-config" works

Tailscale devices discover each other and negotiate direct links through NAT and firewalls without you opening ports. When a direct path is impossible, traffic falls back to an encrypted DERP relay — so connectivity is graceful, not fragile.2

Key idea Your whole private network is a tailnet. Adding a device is "log in", not "allocate an IP and edit a firewall". The control plane shares keys and policy; it is not in the data path.2
