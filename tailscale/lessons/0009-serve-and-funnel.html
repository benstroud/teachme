Serve & Funnel

Exposing a local service to your tailnet, or deliberately to the world.

The mental model in one sentence

Serve exposes a service running on a node to your tailnet over HTTPS; Funnel exposes it to the public internet — both without opening inbound firewall ports or running your own reverse proxy.1

Serve vs Funnel

ServeFunnel
Reachable byYour tailnet devices1Anyone on the internet2
Typical useShare a dev server with your teamA public webhook or demo
TLS certTailscale provisions a valid HTTPS cert for the node name3

Commands

# serve a local port or file to the tailnet
tailscale serve --bg 3000

# expose it to the public internet
tailscale funnel --bg 3000

# see what is shared
tailscale serve status

Tailscale issues the TLS certificate for the *.ts.net name, so URLs come out as real HTTPS with no manual certs.3

Key idea Serve keeps a service private to people you already trust; Funnel deliberately opens it to the world. Choose Funnel last, and only for services designed to be public.2
