Reliability, Order & Replay

The hard quarter: out-of-order and redelivered events, dead letters, and rebuilding state from the bus.

The mental model in one sentence

Assume at-least-once: an event may arrive twice and out of order; make consumers idempotent, drain failures to a DLQ, and lean on replay to rebuild state after an outage.1

Ordering & redelivery

  • Even-per-source ordering is not something to assume globally across producers; events from one stream can arrive nearly together.
  • Consumers should tolerate an OrderUpdated landing before OrderPlaced, or the same event twice.
  • Dedupe on the idempotency key; prefer setting/replacing state over incrementing it so repeats are safe.

Dead letters and replay

  • DLQ — after retries, park the failing event so nothing is silently lost.1
  • Replay — resend past events to rebuild a consumer or recover after a failure.1
Key idea Reliability flips the design: don't chase "exactly once"; embrace at-least-once + idempotent consumers + DLQ + replay. That combination is what makes the pipeline durable.
